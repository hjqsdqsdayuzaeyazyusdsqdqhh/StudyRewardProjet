Clinical Trial Injury Compensation and Insurance

Understanding clinical trial injury compensation and insurance in the US: what is covered, your legal options, and what to expect as a participant.

By Attorney Rachel Green May 13, 2026

Understanding clinical trial injury compensation and insurance in the US: what is covered, your legal options, and what to expect as a participant.

What Happens If You Are Injured in a Clinical Trial

Clinical trial sponsors are generally responsible for providing medical care for injuries directly caused by study participation. The informed consent document should explain whether the sponsor provides free medical treatment for research-related injuries. Some sponsors carry clinical trial insurance that covers injury-related medical costs. However, sponsors may not cover treatment for injuries caused by your underlying condition or failure to follow study instructions.

Understanding the Consent Form's Injury Statement

The informed consent document must include a statement about injury compensation. It will specify whether the sponsor offers free medical treatment, financial compensation, or both for research-related injuries. Some forms state that the sponsor provides no compensation beyond what is required by law. Read this section carefully and ask questions if anything is unclear. The wording varies significantly between sponsors and studies.

Types of Injuries Covered

Research-related injuries can include adverse drug reactions, complications from procedures, equipment malfunctions, and injuries from study-specific tests. Injuries that occur because you did not follow study instructions may not be covered. Pre-existing conditions that worsen naturally during the study are typically not considered research-related injuries. The research team should explain how they distinguish research-related injuries from ordinary disease progression.

Legal Options If You Are Injured

If you experience a research-related injury, first notify the study coordinator immediately to receive appropriate medical care. Document everything about the injury, including when it occurred and how it relates to study procedures. You may have the right to file a legal claim if the injury resulted from negligence or protocol violations. Consult with an attorney experienced in research injury cases to understand your legal options. Many states have specific laws governing clinical trial injury claims.

Key Takeaways

  • Study sponsors typically provide medical care for research-related injuries, but coverage varies by study.
  • The informed consent document must explain injury compensation policies; read this section carefully.
  • Document any injury thoroughly and notify your coordinator immediately for proper care and reporting.
  • You may have legal options if an injury results from negligence; consult an attorney experienced in research cases.
