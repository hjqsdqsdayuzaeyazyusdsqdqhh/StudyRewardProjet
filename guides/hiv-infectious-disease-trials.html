HIV and Infectious Disease Clinical Trials

Overview of clinical trials for HIV, emerging infectious diseases, and antimicrobial resistance research.

By Dr. Emily Chen March 10, 2026

Overview of clinical trials for HIV, emerging infectious diseases, and antimicrobial resistance research.

HIV Treatment and Prevention Research

HIV clinical trials continue to advance treatment and prevention options. Long-acting injectable antiretroviral therapy is a major focus, offering alternatives to daily oral medication. Cure research explores latency reversal agents, immune-based therapies, and gene editing approaches. Pre-exposure prophylaxis or PrEP trials evaluate new formulations including long-acting injectables and implantable devices. Studies also address HIV-related comorbidities and aging with HIV.

Emerging Infectious Disease Studies

The COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated the critical importance of clinical trials for emerging infections. Rapid vaccine development platforms including mRNA and viral vector technologies continue to be refined. Therapeutic trials for emerging viral infections test antivirals, immune modulators, and antibody-based treatments. Pandemic preparedness research establishes trial infrastructure that can be rapidly activated for future outbreaks.

Antimicrobial Resistance Research

Clinical trials combatting antimicrobial resistance explore new antibiotic classes, combination therapies, and alternative approaches. Bacteriophage therapy studies treat drug-resistant infections. Research evaluates rapid diagnostic tests to guide targeted antibiotic use. Stewardship trials examine strategies to preserve existing antibiotic effectiveness while improving patient outcomes.

Tuberculosis and Neglected Disease Trials

TB clinical trials investigate shorter treatment regimens for drug-sensitive and drug-resistant tuberculosis. New diagnostic approaches including molecular testing and imaging are being validated. Research also addresses TB prevention through improved vaccines and prophylactic regimens. Neglected tropical disease trials cover conditions including malaria, dengue, leishmaniasis, and Chagas disease.

Key Takeaways

  • HIV research focuses on long-acting therapies, cure strategies, and improved prevention options including injectable PrEP.
  • Emerging infectious disease trials have been accelerated by mRNA and viral vector vaccine platform technologies.
  • Antimicrobial resistance research explores new antibiotics, bacteriophage therapy, and stewardship strategies.
  • TB trials investigate shorter treatment regimens, better diagnostics, and improved prevention approaches.
