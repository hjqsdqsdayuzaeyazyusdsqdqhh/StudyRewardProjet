Eye Health and Vision Clinical Trials

Guide to clinical trials for eye conditions including macular degeneration, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and cataract research.

By Dr. Emily Chen March 13, 2026

Guide to clinical trials for eye conditions including macular degeneration, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and cataract research.

Age-Related Macular Degeneration Research

AMD clinical trials cover both dry and wet forms of the disease. Wet AMD research continues with new anti-VEGF agents offering longer dosing intervals and improved outcomes. Dry AMD trials have advanced with complement inhibitors and other emerging therapies showing promise. Studies explore combination approaches targeting multiple disease pathways. Geographic atrophy, the advanced form of dry AMD, has seen encouraging trial results with new treatments.

Glaucoma Clinical Trials

Glaucoma research focuses on intraocular pressure reduction through new medication classes, laser techniques, and surgical innovations. Neuroprotection trials aim to preserve optic nerve function beyond pressure control. Minimally invasive glaucoma surgery or MIGS devices are being evaluated for safety and efficacy. Studies also examine adherence strategies and monitoring technologies including home tonometry.

Diabetic Retinopathy Studies

Clinical trials for diabetic retinopathy explore early intervention to prevent vision loss. Anti-VEGF therapy studies continue with refined treatment protocols and longer-acting agents. Research examines optimal screening intervals using artificial intelligence and telemedicine approaches. Studies also investigate systemic risk factor management including intensive glucose and blood pressure control for eye health.

Cataract and Refractive Surgery Research

Cataract trials study new intraocular lens technologies including multifocal, toric, and extended depth of focus lenses. Surgical technique comparisons evaluate femtosecond laser-assisted versus traditional cataract surgery. Refractive surgery research covers advances in LASIK, PRK, and lens-based procedures. Studies also explore treatments for posterior capsule opacification and other post-surgical complications.

Key Takeaways

  • AMD research includes longer-acting anti-VEGF agents for wet AMD and emerging complement inhibitors for dry AMD.
  • Glaucoma trials explore new medications, MIGS devices, and neuroprotection strategies beyond pressure control.
  • Diabetic retinopathy studies focus on early intervention, longer-acting therapies, and AI-based screening.
  • Cataract research evaluates new IOL technologies and surgical technique comparisons.
