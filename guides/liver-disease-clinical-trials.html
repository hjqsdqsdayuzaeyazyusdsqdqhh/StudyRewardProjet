Liver Disease Clinical Trials

Guide to liver disease clinical trials including fatty liver, hepatitis, cirrhosis, and liver cancer research studies.

By Dr. James Wilson March 28, 2026

Guide to liver disease clinical trials including fatty liver, hepatitis, cirrhosis, and liver cancer research studies.

Fatty Liver Disease Research

Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease or NAFLD and its more advanced form NASH are major areas of clinical research. Trials explore medications targeting metabolic pathways, inflammation, and fibrosis. Lifestyle intervention studies examine the effects of diet, exercise, and weight loss on liver health. The NASH research pipeline is particularly active with several Phase 3 trials underway. Non-invasive monitoring tools like elastography are increasingly used to measure treatment effects.

Hepatitis Clinical Trials

Hepatitis B and C research has evolved dramatically with curative treatments now available for hepatitis C. Current hepatitis B trials focus on functional cure strategies including novel antivirals and immunomodulatory approaches. Hepatitis D research is an emerging area with new treatment options being studied. Studies also examine long-term outcomes in patients who have achieved viral suppression, including liver cancer risk reduction.

Cirrhosis and Liver Failure Studies

Clinical trials for cirrhosis address complication management, fibrosis reversal, and prevention of disease progression. Research covers portal hypertension treatments, hepatic encephalopathy management, and strategies to reduce hospitalization. Studies explore biomarkers for early detection of decompensation and novel therapies for acute-on-chronic liver failure. Liver regeneration research aims to reduce the need for transplantation.

Liver Cancer Clinical Trials

Hepatocellular carcinoma research includes trials of targeted therapies, immunotherapies, and combination approaches. Locoregional therapy studies examine optimal techniques for chemoembolization and ablation. Trials also investigate surveillance strategies for early detection in high-risk populations. Adjuvant therapy studies explore treatments to prevent recurrence after curative interventions.

Key Takeaways

  • NAFLD and NASH research is active with multiple Phase 3 trials targeting metabolic and inflammatory pathways.
  • Hepatitis B research focuses on functional cure strategies while hepatitis C trials have shifted to long-term outcomes.
  • Cirrhosis trials address complication management, fibrosis reversal, and prevention of disease progression.
  • Liver cancer research includes targeted therapies, immunotherapies, and optimized locoregional techniques.

Frequently Asked Questions

Many liver trials have traditionally required biopsies, but non-invasive alternatives like elastography and blood biomarkers are increasingly accepted.
Some trials specifically enroll patients with cirrhosis, while others exclude advanced liver disease. Eligibility depends on the study's specific criteria.
NAFLD trials often include dietary modifications, increased physical activity, and weight loss goals as part of the intervention or run-in period.
