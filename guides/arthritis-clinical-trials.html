Arthritis Clinical Trials: Rheumatoid and Psoriatic Arthritis

Guide to arthritis clinical trials including rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, and emerging treatment approaches.

By Dr. James Wilson March 19, 2026

Guide to arthritis clinical trials including rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, and emerging treatment approaches.

Rheumatoid Arthritis Research Advances

Rheumatoid arthritis clinical trials continue to evolve with new treatment options beyond traditional DMARDs and TNF inhibitors. Current research explores JAK inhibitors, BTK inhibitors, and other targeted small molecules. Studies investigate treat-to-target strategies and the optimal timing of biologic therapy initiation. Combination therapy trials examine whether pairing different mechanisms of action improves outcomes. Remission induction and maintenance strategies are also active research areas.

Psoriatic Arthritis Clinical Trials

Psoriatic arthritis research covers both skin and joint manifestations of the disease. Trials of IL-17, IL-23, and JAK inhibitors have expanded treatment options significantly. Studies examine whether treating skin disease early can prevent the development of psoriatic arthritis. Research also focuses on identifying predictors of treatment response to guide therapy selection. Axial involvement in psoriatic arthritis is a growing area of investigation.

Non-Pharmacologic Approaches

Clinical trials also study non-drug interventions for arthritis management. Exercise programs, physical therapy approaches, and dietary interventions are evaluated for their ability to reduce pain and improve function. Acupuncture, mind-body therapies, and other complementary approaches are studied as adjuncts to standard treatment. Weight loss interventions are particularly relevant for patients with osteoarthritis and overweight.

Emerging Treatment Targets

Researchers are exploring novel therapeutic targets including nerve growth factor inhibition for pain management, senolytic drugs that target aging cells contributing to osteoarthritis, and regenerative approaches using stem cells. Cartilage repair and joint preservation studies are advancing for osteoarthritis. Personalized medicine approaches using genetic and biomarker profiling aim to match patients with the most effective treatments.

Key Takeaways

  • Rheumatoid arthritis research includes JAK inhibitors, treat-to-target strategies, and remission optimization approaches.
  • Psoriatic arthritis trials cover IL-17, IL-23, and JAK inhibitors with focus on both skin and joint outcomes.
  • Non-pharmacologic research examines exercise, diet, and complementary therapies for arthritis management.
  • Emerging targets include nerve growth factor inhibitors, senolytic drugs, and regenerative approaches.
