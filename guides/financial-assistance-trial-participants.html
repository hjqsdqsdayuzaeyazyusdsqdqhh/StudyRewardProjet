Financial Assistance Programs for Trial Participants

Resources and programs that provide financial assistance to clinical trial participants, including travel grants and support funds.

By Michael Park May 22, 2026

Resources and programs that provide financial assistance to clinical trial participants, including travel grants and support funds.

Travel and Lodging Assistance Programs

Several organizations provide travel and lodging assistance for clinical trial participants. The Lazarex Cancer Foundation offers travel assistance for cancer patients participating in clinical trials. Angel Airlines for Life and other medical travel charities provide discounted or free airfare. Corporate Angels arranges free air transportation using empty seats on corporate aircraft. The American Cancer Society's Hope Lodge program offers free lodging for cancer patients traveling for treatment, including trial participation.

Condition-Specific Financial Support

Many disease-specific foundations offer financial assistance for research participants. The National Kidney Foundation, American Heart Association, and Alzheimer's Association provide various support programs. Patient advocacy organizations often maintain emergency assistance funds for participants in clinical trials. The HealthWell Foundation and Patient Access Network Foundation offer copay assistance that may apply to trial-related costs.

Sponsor-Provided Support

Some clinical trial sponsors offer direct financial support beyond standard compensation. This may include mileage reimbursement, meal per diems, and childcare reimbursement during study visits. Ask your coordinator about all available support programs during the enrollment process. Sponsors of later-phase trials especially may have established participant support programs to improve retention.

Navigating Financial Assistance Resources

Start by asking your study coordinator about programs they are aware of for your specific study. Search online for clinical trial financial assistance plus your condition name. Contact patient advocacy organizations for your condition, as they often maintain lists of financial resources. Keep records of all trial-related expenses including receipts for travel, lodging, meals, and parking, as some may be reimbursable.

Key Takeaways

  • Travel assistance programs like Lazarex, Angel Airlines, and Hope Lodge provide transportation and lodging support.
  • Condition-specific foundations and patient advocacy organizations offer emergency funds and financial aid for trial participants.
  • Study sponsors may provide additional support including mileage, meals, and childcare reimbursement.
  • Start with your coordinator, then explore advocacy organizations and sponsor resources for financial help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Many programs have income eligibility requirements. However, some assistance programs are available to all participants regardless of income.
Some studies offer childcare reimbursement or assistance. Ask your coordinator about available support for dependent care during visits.
Application processes vary by program. Generally you need to provide study information, travel dates, and documentation of your participation. Start applications well before your travel date.
