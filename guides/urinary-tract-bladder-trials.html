Urinary Tract and Bladder Clinical Trials

Guide to clinical trials for urinary tract conditions including bladder cancer, UTIs, incontinence, and interstitial cystitis.

By Dr. James Wilson March 7, 2026

Guide to clinical trials for urinary tract conditions including bladder cancer, UTIs, incontinence, and interstitial cystitis.

Bladder Cancer Research

Bladder cancer clinical trials cover the full spectrum from non-muscle invasive to metastatic disease. Immunotherapy trials including BCG combinations and checkpoint inhibitors are active for early-stage disease. Studies of antibody-drug conjugates and targeted therapies are advancing treatment for advanced bladder cancer. Research also explores novel intravesical therapies, photodynamic therapy, and risk-adapted surveillance strategies.

Urinary Tract Infection Studies

UTI clinical trials address both uncomplicated and complicated infections. Research on recurrent UTIs includes vaccine development, probiotic approaches, and non-antibiotic prophylactic strategies. Studies of novel antibiotics target drug-resistant uropathogens. Post-menopausal UTI trials explore hormonal and microbiome-based interventions. Catheter-associated UTI research focuses on prevention through device innovation.

Incontinence and Overactive Bladder Research

Clinical trials for urinary incontinence include behavioral, pharmacological, and surgical interventions. Overactive bladder studies evaluate new anticholinergic medications, beta-3 agonists, and neuromodulation approaches. Stress incontinence research examines sling procedures, bulking agents, and pelvic floor training. Mixed incontinence trials address the challenge of treating both urge and stress components.

Interstitial Cystitis and Pelvic Pain Studies

Interstitial cystitis or bladder pain syndrome clinical trials explore treatments including oral medications, bladder instillations, and neuromodulation. Research investigates the role of diet, stress, and pelvic floor dysfunction in symptom management. Studies of novel therapies including platelet-rich plasma and stem cell treatments are emerging. Multidisciplinary treatment approaches for chronic pelvic pain are also being evaluated.

Key Takeaways

  • Bladder cancer research includes immunotherapy, antibody-drug conjugates, and novel intravesical therapy approaches.
  • UTI trials explore vaccines, probiotics, and non-antibiotic strategies for recurrent infection prevention.
  • Incontinence research covers pharmacological, behavioral, and surgical interventions for both urge and stress types.
  • Interstitial cystitis studies examine oral, intravesical, and neuromodulation treatments.
