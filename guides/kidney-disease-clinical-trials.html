Kidney Disease Clinical Trials

Overview of kidney disease clinical trials including chronic kidney disease, dialysis, and kidney transplant research.

By Dr. Emily Chen March 25, 2026

Overview of kidney disease clinical trials including chronic kidney disease, dialysis, and kidney transplant research.

Chronic Kidney Disease Research

Chronic kidney disease clinical trials focus on slowing disease progression, managing complications, and improving outcomes. Research includes studies of SGLT2 inhibitors, which have shown promise in preserving kidney function. Trials explore novel agents targeting fibrosis, inflammation, and metabolic pathways. Blood pressure management studies continue to refine treatment targets. Anemia management research covers new erythropoiesis-stimulating agents and iron formulations.

Dialysis and Renal Replacement Studies

Clinical trials for dialysis patients address both hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis optimization. Research includes new dialyzer technologies, alternative dialysis schedules, and home dialysis approaches. Studies examine vascular access outcomes, infection prevention, and cardiovascular risk reduction in dialysis patients. Trials also explore wearable and implantable artificial kidney technologies that could transform renal replacement therapy.

Kidney Transplant Clinical Trials

Transplant research focuses on improving graft survival and reducing immunosuppression-related complications. Studies explore novel immunosuppressive regimens, tolerance induction strategies, and rejection monitoring tools. Trials investigate antibody-mediated rejection treatments and desensitization protocols for highly sensitized patients. Living donor research includes long-term safety monitoring and surgical technique optimization.

Patient Considerations for Kidney Studies

Kidney clinical trials often involve frequent blood and urine testing to monitor kidney function. Participants may need to maintain stable medication regimens during the study. Some trials require dietary modifications including protein, sodium, and fluid restrictions. Discuss any planned participation with your nephrologist to ensure the study aligns with your overall care plan.

Key Takeaways

  • CKD research focuses on SGLT2 inhibitors, novel antifibrotic agents, and complication management strategies.
  • Dialysis trials explore new technologies, alternative schedules, and home-based approaches including artificial kidney development.
  • Transplant research aims to improve graft survival through novel immunosuppression and rejection monitoring.
  • Kidney studies require frequent lab monitoring and may include dietary modifications.
